## Bounded fresh PAYMENT confirmation and retry

The [v2 amendment](superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-billing-confirmation-retry-v2.md) is implemented for fresh PAYMENT grants selected before any v1 secret binding. GET, polling and command replay never grant confirm or next-action permission. Browser success alone creates no Payment, journal entry, callback or access.

A successor requires the original synchronous card-decline receipt, request identity and exact failed Charge, plus the unchanged original invoice/allocation/PaymentIntent graph, absence of cash and current authority/policy/revision/expiry. An explicit Retry consumes that durable proof once and creates at most one successor on the same PI; fresh details and a separate explicit Pay are then required. A lost HTTP acknowledgement can be recovered by read-only status only when the decline was durably recorded. Lost provider receipt, UNKNOWN/DISPATCHING, unresolved or unbound 3DS failure, policy drift and terminal operation closure cannot create permission. No new invoice, subscription, PI or invoice.pay is introduced by this retry path. Legacy browser-bound or consumed grants cannot migrate or reopen, and SETUP remains v1.
