## Review Focus

- A descriptive or neighboring preparation must never select credentials, locks, target or provider receipt before full original identity authentication; task1 tests forged/swapped context before proof, task2 tests zero client/SDK work.
- A committed firstclaim and a provisional V38 preparation are different branch decisions; task1 tests both opposite method/legacy recovery orders and permanent exclusion after terminal states, including actual legacy guarded loser cleanup without stopping the winner.
- Same-thread timestamps and a synchronized holder do not prove committed caller ownership; task1 tests genuine rollback, independent xid checks and both foreign-pool auto-commit modes; task2 positive composition proves committed claim/ACK/READY sequence.
- A late or ambiguous SDK/storage result must not grant another call or undo READY; task1 covers positive precommit expiry/rollback and identity-only cleanup, task2 covers caller Error/interrupt, late workers and delayed successful READY return.
- SDK getters can coerce malformed wire types or hide duplicate keys; task2 checks strict raw envelope before typed evidence, distinct malformed fields/status/detail combinations and exact immutable clock identity.
